# AHRN Regulatory AI Disclosure

## Purpose of AI
AHRN utilizes Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enhance home reliability through predictive failure detection and bid evaluation reasoning. This technology is intended to assist homeowners and technicians in making data-informed decisions.

## Human-in-the-Loop Policy
Every AI-generated recommendation or diagnostic brief is subject to human review. AI does not have the authority to unilaterally approve financial claims or deny coverage.

## Data Usage and Privacy
- IoT data is used solely for reliability analysis and performance verification.
- PII is anonymized before being processed by Large Language Models.
- Homeowners have the right to request an explanation for any AI-influenced decision.

## Opt-Out
Homeowners may opt-out of "Auto-Claim" features at any time via the Privacy settings in the AHRN Dashboard.

## Oversight
All AI activities are logged and auditable by the AHRN Governance Hub.
